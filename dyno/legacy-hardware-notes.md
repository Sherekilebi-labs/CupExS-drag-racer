# Drag racer

## Hardware components:

- ESP32 dev module
- ta6586 H bridge
- ACS712 current sensor module 30A max current version
- DC motor
- 3S 450mah 50C lipo battery
- DC-DC 12 to 5 volt buck converter

## esp32 pinout wiring

- Motor forward --- gpio 22
- motor backward --- gpio 23
- acs712 out pin --- gpio 35
